    Method & limits
    • Fee: per-minute volume (GeckoTerminal) × pool fee rate × (1 − protocol + fund cut from AmmConfig), split pro-rata across in-range positions.
    • LVR: at every fair-price step, r² × L × √P / 4 (Milionis et al., CLMM in-range form). L is the summed liquidity of positions in range at that price; at the current tick it matches the pool's L exactly.
    • Historical fair price: the reference path named by each pool's badge (e.g. SPY 5-minute prints with overnight gaps filled by ES futures, bad prints that revert immediately dropped) × multiplier + pending dividend.
    • Sessions: Pyth's session calendar (market_session_schedule), including holidays and early closes.
    • Gate: pass only when the lower bound of the 95% bootstrap CI of daily net is above zero and stays above zero with the single best day removed.
    • Uncollected fees: read live from mainnet (PersonalPositionState + PoolState + both TickArrays, Uniswap-v3 fee-growth-inside), valued at the pool price with USDC at $1.
    • Would it pass?: builds the real assert_tradable instruction and runs simulateTransaction on devnet with the keeper as fee payer (nothing is signed). The verdict, compute units and log line come from the program.
    • Limits: the position set is today's snapshot (no open/close history). The pool's own minute prices are not used because they are noisy (±25/±50bp jumps).
