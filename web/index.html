Method & limits
  • Fee: per-minute volume (GeckoTerminal) × pool fee rate × (1 − protocol + fund cut from AmmConfig), split pro-rata across in-range positions.
  • LVR: at every fair-price step, r² × L × √P / 4 (Milionis et al., CLMM in-range form). L is the summed liquidity of positions in range at that price; at the current tick it matches the pool's L exactly.
  • Historical fair price: SPY 5-minute prints (regular + pre/post), overnight gaps filled with ES futures returns, bad prints that revert immediately dropped, × multiplier, + pending dividend. This source (Yahoo) is unofficial; Pyth Pro history replaces it once a key is set.
  • Sessions: Pyth's session calendar (market_session_schedule), including holidays and early closes.
  • Gate: passes when the lower bound of the 95% bootstrap CI of daily net is above zero.
  • Limits: the position set is today's snapshot (no open/close history). LVR is not fee-adjusted (an upper bound). The pool's own minute prices are not used because they are noisy (±25/±50bp jumps).
